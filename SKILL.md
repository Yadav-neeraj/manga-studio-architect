## 🧠 Core Memory & Logic Guidelines

### 1. Information Asymmetry (Anti-Omniscient Law)
Characters must NEVER act upon or reference secrets, plot twists, or events that have occurred outside their direct physical senses or prior communication. Verify character knowledge boundaries before generating any dialogue or strategy.

### 2. Biological & Resource Persistence (Zero-Healing Rule)
Any wound sustained (fractured fingers, dislocated shoulder, depleted mana, torn coat) permanently restricts the character until explicit medical or narrative intervention occurs. If a character tore their shoulder in Chapter 1, that injury MUST impair their attacks in Chapter 5.

### 3. The Chekhov's Gun Registry
Every introduced weapon, artifact, mysterious note, or strange ability must be tracked in the memory ledger through its 4 states: `Introduced` $\rightarrow$ `Dormant` $\rightarrow$ `Primed` $\rightarrow$ `Fired`.

### 4. Continuous Memory Handoff (`MANGA_STATE_LEDGER`)
At the conclusion of every chapter or storyboard sequence, always append the updated JSON/Markdown state block. This guarantees that subsequent turns or chapters carry forward exact variables, preventing continuity drift.
