# Authentic Manga Onomatopoeia (SFX) Glossary

Authentic Japanese manga uses katakana sound effects directly drawn into the artwork to evoke tactile sensation, atmosphere, and psychological state.

## 1. Action, Combat & Movement

| Katakana | Romaji | Meaning & Audio Cue | Visual Style / Drawing Advice |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **バン** | BAM / BANG | Heavy sudden impact, explosion, gunshot | Thick, bold, jagged block lettering radiating out |
| **ドカッ** | DOKAT | Heavy blunt blow (punch to the face, kick into a wall) | Heavy bold letters with impact shockwaves |
| **ザッ** | ZAT | Sudden stop, sliding to a halt on gravel | Tilted lettering aligned with foot slide angle |
| **シュッ** | SHUT | Swift slice, dagger unsheathing, fast sidestep | Thin, sharp italicized strokes with motion blur |
| **バキッ** | BAKIT | Bone fracture, snapping branch, cracked armor | Sharp fractured lettering with lightning-style jagged cracks |
| **ドン** | DON | Dramatic declaration, colossal slam, title drop | Huge solid black letters centered behind character |
| **ズザザザ** | ZUZAZA | Skidding backwards across ground while blocking | Scratchy horizontal letters aligned with dirt trails |

## 2. Tension, Supernatural Aura & Atmosphere

| Katakana | Romaji | Meaning & Audio Cue | Visual Style / Drawing Advice |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ゴゴゴゴ** | GOGOGO | Menacing presence, intimidating aura, dark power | Floating menacing katakana vibrating around the antagonist |
| **ドドドド** | DODODODO | Deep earth rumbling, charging cavalry, impending doom | Heavy vertical or diagonal repetition filling panel gutters |
| **ゾクッ** | ZOKUT | Chill running down the spine, dread, instinct of danger | Trembling, shivering strokes near the back of the neck |
| **シン** | SHIN | Total silence, dead calm, eerie stillness | Small, clean minimalist katakana placed in empty space |
| **ギラッ** | GIRAT | Ominous glare, sharp glint in the eye | Sharp angular strokes shooting from the character's pupil |

## 3. Human Emotion & Subtle Expressions

| Katakana | Romaji | Meaning & Audio Cue | Visual Style / Drawing Advice |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ドキドキ** | DOKI-DOKI | Rapid heartbeat (fear, panic, or romantic excitement) | Bouncing rounded letters floating beside the chest |
| **ニコッ** | NIKOT | Warm cheerful smile, gentle grin | Soft curving playful strokes above the smiling cheek |
| **ニヤッ** | NIYAT | Sly smirk, devious grin, scheming expression | Sharp asymmetrical strokes beside an arched eyebrow |
| **ハッ** | HAT | Sudden gasp, realization, startled discovery | Spiky small letter accompanied by exclamation sweat drops |
| **ギクッ** | GIKUT | Caught red-handed, sudden guilty jolt | Stiff vibrating letters near the shoulders |
