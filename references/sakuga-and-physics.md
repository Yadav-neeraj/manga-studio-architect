# Sakuga Animation Principles & Combat Physics Engine

To elevate static comic panels into animated cinematic sequences (Sakuga / 作画), every action must obey core principles of motion animation and visceral physics.

---

## 1. The Animation Principles Translated to Sequential Art

### A. Squash & Stretch (Elastic Anatomy)
* **During Acceleration:** The human figure or weapon stretches and elongates along the motion vector. Linework becomes razor-sharp, thin, and streamlined.
* **During Collision (Impact):** The target’s face, armor, or muscle physically squashes, compressing inward against bone or bedrock before snapping back. Show cheeks deforming, saliva spraying tangentially, and ribs flattening under the fist.

### B. Anticipation (The Visual Coil)
Never draw an attack without showing the preceding compression.
* If a character is about to deliver a world-shattering punch, the previous panel MUST show:
  - Weight dropped onto the back heel (heel gouging into the dirt).
  - Torso twisted like a loaded spring (clothes creased tightly around the waist).
  - Eyes locked onto the target with absolute dilated focus.
* *Rule:* The deeper the anticipation coil, the more devastating the reader perceives the resulting impact to be.

### C. Follow-Through & Overlapping Action
When the primary body stops moving, secondary elements MUST continue along the original momentum vector:
* Hair strands, coat hems, neckties, hanging chains, and belt straps whip forward.
* Expelled blood, sparks, or shattered rock fragments fly ahead of the stopped fist.

### D. Smears & Multi-Limb Afterimages
To convey speeds faster than human visual processing (60fps blur in static ink):
* Draw **smear panels**: A single blade drawn as 5 overlapping ghost silhouettes with faded ink washes.
* Draw the hand not as fingers, but as an elongated streak of speedlines connecting the start position to the end position in one continuous brush stroke.

## 2. The Combat Physics Engine

### A. Kinetic Transfer & Recoil Conservation
* **Newton's Third Law in Ink:** If a hero punches a 10-ton monster, the ground beneath the hero's back foot MUST buckle and crack backward to counteract the equal-and-opposite recoil.
* If a strike does not shatter the ground behind the striker, it does not look like a heavy punch.

### B. Atmospheric Shockwave Mechanics
* **Mach Cones & Vapor Rings:** Supersonic strikes compress moisture in the air into a white conical vapor shockwave (Prandtl-Glauert singularity).
* **Vacuum Cavitation:** Behind a high-speed strike, draw air rushing inward to fill the low-pressure pocket, pulling gravel, dust, and loose leaves along the wake.

### C. "Yutapon Cubes" (Nakamura Debris Physics)
Named after legendary Bones animator Yutaka Nakamura:
* When a colossal impact strikes concrete or stone, do not draw generic dust clouds.
* Draw geometric **square and cubical chunks of debris** (Yutapon cubes) violently erupting upward and hovering weightlessly in mid-air for a split second due to the sheer velocity of the shockwave.

### D. Impact Frames (The Negative Flash)
* A high-impact panel drawn with **inverted contrast**: Pitch-black silhouettes against a blinding stark white background, or white anatomical X-ray silhouettes with jagged lightning veins.
* Represents a strike so explosive that it "burns" the visual sensorium. Used exclusively for turning-point hits (e.g., *Black Flash* in JJK, *United States of Smash* in MHA).
