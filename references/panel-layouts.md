# Manga & Webtoon Paneling Masterclass

## 1. Traditional Manga Layout Principles (Gyaku-Z Reading Flow)

Traditional Japanese manga reads from **Right to Left, Top to Bottom**. The reader's eye naturally sweeps in a reversed "Z" pattern.

### The Golden Ratio of Page Layouts
- **Panel Count:** 4 to 6 panels per regular dialogue page; 2 to 4 panels per high-action page; 1 panel (Splash Page) or 2 panels (Double-page Spread) for climactic moments.
- **Hierarchical Panel Sizing:**
  - **Dominant Panel (Focal Point):** Occupies 40-50% of page height. Contains the most significant event, emotion, or action of the page.
  - **Secondary Panels:** 20-30% height each. Setup, reaction, or progressive beats.
  - **Tertiary / Inset Panels:** Small floating boxes showing micro-expressions, hands tightening, or eye shifts.

### Panel Shape Psychology
| Shape | Visual Emotional Impact | Best Used For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Wide Horizontal** | Calming, cinematic, time expansion | Establishing shots, sweeping landscapes, staring down an opponent |
| **Tall Vertical** | Dominance, falling, towering height | Looking up at giant foes, skydives, rising energy blasts |
| **Slanted / Diagonal** | Speed, tension, unstable crisis | Sword slashes, martial arts kicks, sudden car swerves |
| **Borderless / Bleed** | Overwhelming power, borderless time | Shocking revelations, catastrophic explosions, memory flashbacks |

## 2. Webtoon (Vertical Scrolling) Rhythm & Spacing

Webtoons are designed for mobile devices (smartphones). The reading flow is strictly **Vertical (Scroll Down)**.

### The Distance Rule (Gutter as Time)
In webtoons, vertical distance between panels represents elapsed time and emotional breathing room:
- **Tight Gutters (20-40px):** Fast-paced action, rapid-fire dialogue exchanges, frantic combos.
- **Medium Gutters (80-120px):** Standard transition between conversation turns.
- **Deep Gutters (200-400px+):** Prolonged silence, shock, dramatic anticipation before scrolling to reveal a twist.

### Scroll-Reveal Technique (The Mobile Cliffhanger)
- Position an incomplete silhouette or sound effect just above the fold.
- Force the reader to scroll past a blank canvas before slamming them with a full-color vertical splash panel.
