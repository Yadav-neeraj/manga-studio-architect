# The Logic, Deductive Causality & Anti-Plot-Hole Engine

Reverse-engineered from the airtight narrative logic of *Tactical Chess and Causality Master (Hunter x Hunter)*, *Naoki Urasawa (Monster / 20th Century Boys)*, and *Hajime Isayama (Attack on Titan)*.

This engine enforces uncompromising cause-and-effect integrity, ensuring that long-form manga narratives never suffer from contrivances, plot armor, unearned victories, or logical contradictions.

---

## 1. The Four Laws of Narrative Causality

### Law 1: The Information Asymmetry Matrix (Cognitive Isolation)
* **The Flaw:** Characters act on information only the author or reader knows (omniscient leak).
* **The Rule:** Every character's decision MUST be justified purely by their individual sensory field, deductive capability, and past experiences.
* **The Audit:**
  ```markdown
  - Target Character: [Name]
  - Sensory Input in Scene: What did they see, hear, or smell directly?
  - Prior Knowledge: What were they explicitly told earlier?
  - Cognitive Blindspots: What critical truth are they completely unaware of?
  - Inferred Hypothesis: What reasonable (or flawed) assumption are they acting upon?
  ```

### Law 2: Biological & Resource Persistence (Zero-Healing Contradiction)
* **The Flaw:** Characters sustain severe fractures, cuts, or magic depletion in Chapter 1, but by Chapter 3 they are leaping around with zero physical penalty.
* **The Rule:** Every injury, fractured rib, torn ligament, and expended energy unit remains active and degrades performance until explicitly treated by medicine, surgery, or time.
* **The Audit:**
  - Before writing an action (e.g., throwing a hook, sprinting, casting a spell), check the **Character's Active Injury Ledger**.
  - If a character has a shattered right forearm, any punch delivered with that arm will snap the bone further, cause blinding agony, and drastically reduce kinetic velocity.

### Law 3: Togashi's Cost-Benefit Axiom (The Law of Equivalent Exchange)
* **The Flaw:** Characters suddenly unlock a massive new power or miraculous boost simply by screaming about friendship (unearned power-ups).
* **The Rule:** Power in high-stakes manga is mathematically bound by **Risk, Restriction, and Sacrifice**:
  $$\text{Combat Multiplier} = \text{Base Mastery} \times (\text{Severity of Limitation} \times \text{Risk of Permanent Ruin})$$
* If a character gains a 5x speed boost, they must pay a proportional tax (e.g., muscle fibers tearing, reduced visual field, irreversible loss of an organ, or strict activation conditions).

### Law 4: The Chekhov's Gun Lifecycle
* **The Flaw:** Items, weapons, or mysterious artifacts are introduced and then vanish into thin air, or solutions appear out of nowhere (Deus Ex Machina).
* **The Rule:** Every significant narrative object or ability must progress through a 4-stage lifecycle:
  1. **Introduced (Foreshadowed):** Planted naturally in the background without drawing undue attention.
  2. **Dormant:** Mentioned or carried passively in inventory across multiple chapters.
  3. **Primed:** Brought into focus as a potential tactical variable.
  4. **Fired (Resolved):** Used under strict logical constraints to alter the trajectory of a scene.

## 2. Spatial & Geometric Consistency Check
Before executing any multi-character combat or dialogue exchange, map the physical spatial grid:
- **Line-of-Sight Check:** Can Character A physically see Character B through the dust, angle, or terrain?
- **Distance & Travel Time:** If Character A is 50 meters away, they cannot strike Character B in 0.01 seconds without exceeding Mach 15 (which creates an unavoidable sonic shockwave).
- **Collateral Consequences:** Every missed attack (e.g., stray energy blast, severed boulder) must strike an environment feature, permanently altering the terrain for subsequent panels.
